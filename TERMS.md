# Terms of Use (BOX-LOG)

*Last updated: 2026-10-28. Mirrors `terms.html`, which is the Hebrew text members read. If the two ever diverge, the Hebrew governs for members.*

BOX-LOG is a free app, a personal project by Shahaf Rachmany, for private circles of friends. There is no gym, coach or club behind it. Using the app means you accept these terms.

## Free and invite only

No payments and no ads. There is no open sign-up: you join through an invite link from a circle member. The link is valid for a short time and a limited number of uses.

## Your account

You keep your username and password safe. With no email address there is no other way to recover an account, so complete account recovery on the security screen.

## Circles

- A circle is a private group of up to 20 members (a couple: up to 2).
- You choose what to share in each circle. What you share is shown to members of that circle.
- A circle owner sets the circle's settings and can remove a member.

## Fair use

- Do not harass members or impersonate anyone.
- Do not bypass invite limits, rate limits or privacy settings.
- Do not try to reach another circle's data.

## Your content

Your content stays yours. What you share is stored and shown to the circle you chose until you delete it, leave the circle or delete your account.

## Not medical advice

The app records what you enter. It is not medical or professional advice. Train at your own risk.

## As is

This is a personal project and the service is provided as is, with no uptime promise. An account that breaks these terms can be limited or removed, and the service can change or stop. If these terms change, the date above is updated.
