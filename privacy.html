Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-05

This policy explains what information the app collects, why, how it is shared, and what control you have over it. It covers both the private training log (your workouts, saved on your device and backed up to the cloud) and the optional community features (a public-to-the-club profile, posts, comments, follows, and leaderboards).

Who runs this

There is no separate company behind this app. It is run directly by the club's own coaching team for their own members — there is no registered legal entity, business address, or compliance department, and this policy does not claim that any particular country's law or courts govern it. Wherever this policy says "we" or "the operator," it means the coaching team who runs the app and the club. If a disagreement ever came up, it would be worked out directly between you and the coaching team, the same way any other question about the club would be.

For any privacy question, request, or complaint, contact your coach directly — the same way you'd reach them about anything else related to the club. There is no separate support email; the coaching team is the whole of "support" for this app.

Information we collect

  • Account and sign-in data. You can create an account anonymously (no personal information is required) or with a member handle and password. Sign-in never collects an email address or any other contact detail — a community account is identified only by an anonymous session and the profile handle you choose. Where a password login internally requires an email format, the app generates one from your handle; it is not a real address and is never used to contact you.
  • Invite and membership data. If you join through an invite code, we record which code was redeemed, by which account, and when, to enforce one-use and rate-limit rules.
  • Training-log data. Workouts, results, personal records, bodyweight, body measurements, session notes, scaling notes, and partner tags that you enter into the app.
  • Community content. Posts (a workout name, result, date, Rx/Scaled state, and comparison score, plus any photo you attach), comments on posts, reactions, follows, and the display name, handle, and avatar on your profile.
  • Attendance and booking data. Class attendance and upcoming bookings, where the club uses these features, tied to your account.
  • Technical data. Standard device and request metadata generated by using a web app (timestamps, error logs, and the equivalent of browser/device information), used only for reliability and abuse prevention.
  • Engagement and usage analytics. We record which screens you open and which community content you view or interact with (for example, opening the feed, viewing a post, or visiting a profile) — not the training-log data itself, just that an interaction happened and when. This is aggregated into club-wide participation metrics, such as how active the community is week to week. This data is kept for 90 days and then deleted; it is never sold or shared outside the coaching team, and it is not used to make any automated decision about you.
  • Attendance-based coach engagement signal. Separately from the above, your class attendance history (not what you view or click in the app) is used to compute a simple week-to-week attendance rate for you and compare it against your own recent baseline. If that comparison shows your attendance trending down, coaches and admins can see the flag — your baseline rate and your recent rate, not a detailed log — so they can reach out rather than a member quietly drifting away unnoticed. It is not used to make any automated decision about you, only to prompt a human coach to check in, and a coach can dismiss a flag once reviewed. This signal is not currently deleted on a fixed schedule; it persists until a coach reviews or dismisses it.

We do not collect government ID, precise location, financial account numbers, or biometric data, and we do not use any of the above for advertising.

Automatic private backup

New workouts are backed up automatically and privately to the cloud from the first save on — only the athlete can see this data, and it is entirely separate from joining the community (posting, a public profile, the feed). This automatic backup can be turned off at any time in Settings, and turning it off never affects anything already saved on the device itself.

Community sharing

A community post is created only after you select a result and an audience for it — nothing you log is shared with the club automatically. Posts and comments are visible only to whoever the chosen audience allows; bodyweight, body measurements, session notes, scaling notes, partner tags, and backup files are never published to the community regardless of audience. Following another member is visible to that member and, depending on their own privacy settings, to people who can see their profile — it is not anonymous.

You control a set of visibility toggles on your own profile: whether your profile is visible to the club at all, and whether workout results, personal records, achievements, attendance, upcoming bookings, and leaderboard placement are shown to others. Separate toggles control whether other members can follow, @mention, or message you, and whether your birthday is shown. These toggles are enforced at the database level, not only hidden in the app's screens.

Who can see what

  • Other members see only what your audience choice and privacy toggles allow for any given post, profile field, or activity.
  • Club admins can see a directory of every member's handle, display name, role, and join date, for club administration purposes, regardless of a member's own profile-visibility toggles. A club-wide attendance policy set by admins can also make attendance visible to admins even when a member's own attendance toggle is off; it does not override any other privacy toggle.
  • Coaches and admins can also see the attendance-based engagement signal described above (a general "trending down" flag built from your attendance rate, not a log of what you viewed or clicked) — this is separate from, and not limited by, your own profile-visibility toggles, since it exists specifically to support outreach from the people already responsible for the club.
  • We (the coaching team) and our subprocessors below can access data as needed to run, secure, and support the service. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Subprocessors

We use a small number of infrastructure providers to run the service, each bound by their own data-processing terms:

  • Supabase provides our database, authentication, and file storage (including uploaded post photos). Account, training-log, and community data described in this policy is stored and processed there, in Supabase's ap-southeast-1 (Singapore) hosting region.
  • GitHub Pages serves the app's static files (the code that runs in your browser). It does not receive your account or training-log data; that traffic goes directly to Supabase.

Legal basis for processing

Where a legal basis would matter, we think about our handling of your data this way: performance of a contract (to create your account and provide the training log and community features you sign up for), your consent (for optional features such as joining the community, sharing a post, or enabling automatic backup), our legitimate interest (to secure the service, prevent abuse, enforce invite and rate limits, and let coaches identify and support members whose participation is dropping off), and legal obligation where applicable law requires specific records to be retained or disclosed. This mirrors how the app already works rather than resting on any claimed jurisdiction.

Data retention

Your training-log data is kept for as long as your account is active. If you request deletion, your profile and posts are hidden immediately and the account and its content are permanently deleted after 30 days. Beyond that 30-day window, infrastructure-level backups and technical/security logs follow our hosting provider's own standard backup-rotation and log-retention windows — we don't separately extend those windows or track them by an exact day count, and they are not used for anything beyond restoring service or investigating abuse before they age out. The engagement and usage analytics described above follow their own, shorter 90-day window and are deleted on that schedule regardless of account status. The attendance-based coach engagement signal is not on a fixed deletion schedule today; a flag persists until a coach reviews or dismisses it, or until the account itself is deleted per the rule above.

Your rights

Even without a specific law requiring it, you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, correct it, request its deletion, receive a copy of it in a portable format, object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent for anything based on consent (such as community sharing or automatic backup) without affecting anything already processed. In the app, you can export your local data, turn off automatic backup, adjust every privacy toggle listed above, block other members, report content, and request full account deletion at any time. To exercise a right that isn't available directly in the app, contact your coach directly. If a data protection authority is relevant where you live, you can also raise concerns with them.

Children's privacy

This service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under that age. 13 is a general baseline we chose as a reasonable policy, not a number required by a specific law that applies to this app. If you believe a child has created an account, contact your coach directly and we will remove it.

Security

We use industry-standard access controls, encryption in transit, and row-level database policies so that a member can only read or write the data their own permissions and privacy settings allow, enforced by the database itself rather than only by the app's screens. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

International transfers

Regardless of where you access the service from, your information is processed by Supabase in its ap-southeast-1 (Singapore) hosting region. By using the service you understand that your information will be transferred there for processing.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. Material changes will be reflected by an updated date at the top of this document and, where required, an in-app notice.

Contact

Questions, requests, or complaints about this policy can be sent to your coach directly — see "Who runs this" above.
